Forecast summary for 08 July 2026:

A pronounced dry signal persists across much of western and northwestern Kenya throughout the next fortnight, while the eastern highlands trend toward anomalously wet conditions in the later weeks of the forecast.

HIGHLANDS WEST OF THE RIFT VALLEY:

RIFT VALLEY AND LAKE VICTORIA BASIN:

HIGHLANDS EAST OF THE RIFT VALLEY:

NORTHEASTERN KENYA:
Generally dry conditions dominate for the first three weeks, followed by a transition to near-normal or slightly above-normal rainfall toward week 5.

SOUTHEASTERN LOWLANDS:
Week 1 sees a localized wet pulse with 14% of grid cells exceeding EFI 0.5 (max_EFI=0.58, p50anom=+25%, +0.6mm), followed by a return to near-normal conditions for the remainder of the period.

COAST:
A consistent trend of above-normal rainfall is expected, strengthening gradually from week 2 (p50anom=+22%, +1.6mm) through week 6 (p50anom=+36%, +2.7mm).

Overall, the six-week outlook indicates a shift from a broad dry anomaly affecting the western and northern sectors toward a wetter pattern developing over the eastern highlands and coast.
