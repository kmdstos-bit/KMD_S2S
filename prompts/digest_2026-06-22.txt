Forecast summary for 22 June 2026:

A distinct spatial divide characterizes this outlook, with a persistent dry signal dominating the western half of the country, while the Highlands East of the Rift Valley and the coast exhibit a tendency toward wetter-than-normal conditions.

These regions face a significant dry signal. Northwestern Kenya is particularly impacted, with high ensemble agreement in week 1 (p33=80%, p50anom=-60%, -4.8mm) through week 2 (p33=89%, p50anom=-68%, -6.7mm). COAST:

NORTHEASTERN KENYA AND SOUTHEASTERN LOWLANDS:
Both regions exhibit generally dry conditions in the first two weeks. Following this, the signals become muted and oscillate near climatological norms with no strong leanings for the remainder of the forecast period.

Overall, the six-week outlook indicates a persistent moisture deficit across the western and northwestern sectors of the country, contrasted by a developing wet trend for the eastern highlands and the coastal strip.
