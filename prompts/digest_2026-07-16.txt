COAST AND NORTHEASTERN KENYA:

SOUTHEASTERN LOWLANDS:
This region remains characterized by near-neutral conditions with no strong signals emerging across the six-week horizon.

The overall six-week outlook indicates a deepening drought for western and northwestern Kenya, while the eastern highlands and coastal areas lean toward an increasingly wet regime as the forecast progresses.
