Forecast summary for 24 June 2026:

Highlands east of the Rift Valley face an unusually wet week 1 with significant extreme potential, sharply contrasting with persistent dry conditions prevailing across the western and northwestern sectors of the country.

This region shows a strong wet signal in week 1 (p50anom=+152%, +10.9mm) with 33% of grid cells exceeding an EFI of 0.8, indicating a risk of extreme rainfall. The wet trend remains elevated throughout the six-week period, with further pulses of above-average precipitation suggested in week 3 (p50anom=+109%, +7.4mm).

NORTHEASTERN KENYA AND SOUTHEASTERN LOWLANDS:
These areas exhibit near-normal conditions in the short term, with subtle shifts toward weak above-normal tendencies emerging in the latter half of the forecast period.

COAST:
After a dry week 1 (p50anom=-28%, -2.4mm), the region transitions to a persistent wet tendency from week 2 through week 6, with rainfall anomalies gradually climbing to +50% (+3.7mm) by week 4.

Overall, the six-week outlook depicts a stark moisture split between an anomalously wet eastern highland corridor and an extensively dry western half of the country, with coastal areas trending toward wetter conditions after an initial dry start.
