NORTHEASTERN KENYA AND SOUTHEASTERN LOWLANDS:

The six-week outlook indicates a country-wide prevalence of drier-than-average conditions in the near term, with a notable shift toward increased moisture availability over eastern and coastal regions during the second half of the forecast period.
